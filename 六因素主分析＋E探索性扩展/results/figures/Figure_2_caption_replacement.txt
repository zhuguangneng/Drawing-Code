Figure 2. Increment in registered cancer deaths under the primary six-factor joint model and key sensitivity analyses. Points indicate medians and horizontal lines indicate 95% uncertainty intervals from 100,000 Monte Carlo draws. The primary model combined health-care access barriers, diagnostic delay, health workforce loss or shortage, psychosocial stress, patient–provider communication barriers, and treatment delay or interruption. Particulate-matter exposure was excluded from the primary joint model and added only in the orange exploratory seven-factor extension using Δx=4.1 μg/m³. Full multiplication and additive excess risk are extreme structural explorations.
